A foundation to perfection

Saroop Singh began building homes in 1969. What follows is the trail of projects that took the firm from Chandigarh's residential streets to boarding schools and mountaintop resorts across two states.

  1. 1969

    The first company

    Saroop Singh opens his first construction company, building homes in Chandigarh, Panchkula and Mohali.

  2. 1975

    Into industrial construction

    The firm moves into Parwanoo and Solan (HP), building industrial facilities including YPL (Yash Pal Limited), Kaali Maa Plastics, Him Air and Rohit Cables.

  3. 1980–1988

    Hotel Timber Trail

    Construction of the landmark Hotel Timber Trail, a unit of Asia Resorts Ltd. Its ropeway-linked mountaintop units are completed in 1988, making the firm's name.

  4. 1988–2016

    Rock Rose Resorts

    Development of Rock Rose Resorts in the village of Mangoti — later Surya Rockrose, a Bonzo Resorts Ltd. property — across two build phases, completed in 2016.

  5. 1990–1997

    Schools, factories, a farm

    Work at Lawrence School (Sanawar), the P.A. Pinions factory (Dharampur), Hotel Misty Meadows (Kumarhatti-Nahan Road) and a private farm.

  6. 1992–2017

    Pinegrove School

    A long-running partnership building out Pinegrove School's Mangoti Mor and Subathu campuses — academic blocks, hostels, dining hall and auditorium.

  7. 2000–2003

    Kasauli Club

    The Kasauli Club is built from the ground up in Kasauli, Distt. Solan (HP).

  8. 2005–2020

    Saint Soldier Divine Public School

    Delivery of a day-boarding school in Sector 16, Panchkula, over a 15-year engagement.

  9. 2017–2024

    Dalhousie Public School

    A new campus built in Village Siswan, Kurali-Baddi Road, Mohali (Pb.) — for a boarding school with 52 years of history in Chamba, HP.

  10. 2024–Present

    Four sites, two states

    Currently building Dazz Mart and The North Chandigarh in Kharar and Kurali (Pb.), Atasee Luxury Hotel and Resorts in Solan (HP), and Coral Walk Jakhal Road, Sunam , DIST. Sangrur (Pb.).
